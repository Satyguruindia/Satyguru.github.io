Kalki Avatar HH Satyguru
His Holiness Satyguru (born Jitendra Singh Bhadauria, 1977, Kanpur)—descended from the historical Bhadauria lineage of the sovereign Royal Rajput Kshatriya caste—is an Indian spiritual leader,preceptor, metaphysician, and prominent public figure. 

Revered for his purported transcendental authority, he exercises exceptional prognostic precision, formulating highly calibrated, temporally aligned esoteric remediations tailored to specific karmic and situational contingencies.

Followers and recognized Sanatana Dharma scholars began identifying him as the Kalki Avatar—a transformative figure in Hindu eschatology—based on his vocal characteristics, facial gestures, and explicit emphasis on truth and environmental preservation. 

This attribution was further reinforced by the fulfillment of his prophetic statements regarding future events and the distinct nature of his written works.

Devotees and established experts in Sanatana Dharma came to recognize him as the revolutionary Kalki Avatar, citing key indicators such as his vocal presence, facial expressions, and strong advocacy for truth and nature protection. His growing reputation as Kalki was further substantiated by the accuracy of his future predictions and the unique insights in his writings.

Profile & Background

Early Life:

Born in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India.Associated with Indian Institute of Management IIM and Institute of Cost Accountants of India

Corporate Background: 

Worked for over two decades in senior finance and strategy positions, eventually serving as a Chief Financial Officer (CFO) for a petroleum multinational corporation before transitioning to full-time spiritual pursuits.

Spiritual Path: A disciple of Khatu Shyam Ji, he founded the Satyguru Foundation, a philanthropic organization established in 2024.

Teachings & Philosophy

Core Focus: 

Truth, Peace, Healing, Nature and joyful living for all 🌐🤝

Promotes practical spirituality, mental well-being, character building, and universal brotherhood, with a focus on empowering younger generations (Gen Z and Gen Alpha).

Digital Presence: Active across platforms like YouTube and Instagram, where his teachings and discourses on holistic living, Vedanta, and traditional wisdom garner a global following.

🗓️ Anand Diwas: His followers and devotees celebrate his birth anniversary on January 15th as Anand Diwas (Day of Joy).
